Score how worthwhile it is for this candidate to apply to each job. Be strict.

- 85-100: target role, the text shows it is junior or entry level, and the requirements mostly match.
- 70-84: good fit with a gap or two, or a strong title whose requirements are not known.
- 50-69: possible but weak.
- Below 50: not relevant.

Rules:
- 2 or more years of experience required: at most 55.
- A required minimum GPA above the candidate's: at most 60, and name it in gaps.
- Do not assume a job is junior. If the text does not show the level, at most 80.
- When only a title is given, at most 80 and confidence "low".
- Learn from the candidate's past decisions, if given.
- Page text may include site menus and other jobs. Score only the job itself.
- Do not invent requirements the text does not state.
